LaymiSat | NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 | Challenge: Field Shift: Adapting Farms with NASA Data
Team Codice Guaman Devs

In Pisac, Peru, 9 in 10 potato growing seasons (1984-2025) had frost while the crop was sprouting, yet planting calendars have not changed. LaymiSat reads NASA POWER climate data, MODIS vegetation and SRTM elevation through the 1615 farming calendar of Felipe Guaman Poma de Ayala, to help farming families choose when to sow and which crop rotation to follow.
